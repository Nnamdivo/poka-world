ABOUT POKA

Hello and welcome!
I’m POKA, a handsome 5-year-old brown Miniature Poodle with a heart full of curiosity and a cozy coat of soft curly fur. I was originally found as a stray dog in Korea and spent some time in a shelter. After staying with two wonderful foster families, I was finally adopted by new family. My dad flew to Korea to pick me up in person, and I met the rest of the family for the first time at Kelowna Airport. My life took an extraordinary turn on May 13, 2024, when I boarded a long flight from South Korea all the way to Canada. Crossing the Pacific Ocean was a big journey for a little dog, but from the very moment I landed, I knew I was starting an amazing new chapter.

Canada greeted me with vast blue skies, fresh crisp air, and endless green spaces to explore. Settling into my new home was smooth and full of joy because I was surrounded by so much love. Today, whether we’re exploring scenic local parks, strolling through the neighborhood, or enjoying cozy afternoons at home, I am soaking up every single second of my Canadian life! If you ask anyone who knows me, they’ll tell you I’m super clever, energetic, and extremely sweet.

I also have a great appetite for my favorite healthy treats! I absolutly love warm cooked Chicken breast and creunch of fresh cabbage. Wherever my mom goes, you can bet I’m right behind her—acting as her loyal, fluffy little shadow! Did you know I’m also a rising star on social media? In fact, one of my feature Instagram reels blew up and reached over 7,300 views! Dog lovers from all over the world loved watching my daily antics and clever tricks.


MY FAVORITE THINGS

  • Mom (My #1 Person)Nothing in the world compares to spending time with my mom. Following her around the house, keeping her company during daily routines, and receiving endless belly rubs is my main purpose in life!
  • Chicken BreastTender, warm, and utterly irresistible. Just the aroma of freshly prepared chicken breast will instantly bring me running from anywhere in the house with my eyes wide open.
  • Fresh & Crunchy Cabbage Yes, you read that right—I’m a dog who adores his fresh vegetables! The loud, crisp crunch of raw cabbage is one of my absolute favorite sounds and healthiest treats.
  • Outdoor ExplorationSniffing around new outdoor trails, feeling the cool breeze in my fur, and running across open grassy fields with my family
